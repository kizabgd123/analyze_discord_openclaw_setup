# NeurIPS 2025 Paper Checklist

## Paper Checklist (to be included in appendix)

For most papers, it is recommended to include answers to the following questions in the appendix:

1. **Claims**
   - Q: Do the main claims made in the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope?

   - Justification: [Your justification here]

2. **Limitations**
   - Q: Does the paper discuss the limitations of the work performed by the authors?
   - A: [Yes/No/Not Applicable]
   - Justification: [Your justification here]

3. **Theory Assumptions and Proofs**
   - Q: For each theoretical result, does the paper provide the full set of assumptions and a complete (and correct) proof?
   - A: [Yes/No/Not Applicable]
   - Justification: [Your justification here]

4. **Experimental Result Reproducibility**
   - Q: Does the paper fully disclose all the information needed to reproduce the main experimental results of the paper to the extent that it affects the main claims and/or conclusions of the paper (regardless of whether the code and data are provided or not)?
   - A: [Yes/No/Not Applicable]
   - Justification: [Your justification here]

5. **Open access to data and code**
   - Q: Does the paper provide links to any data or code that is necessary to reproduce the main experimental results?
   - A: [Yes/No/Not Applicable]
   - Justification: [Your justification here]

6. **Experimental Setting/Details**
   - Q: Does the paper specify all the training and test details (e.g., data splits, hyperparameters, how they were chosen, pre-processing software, etc.) needed to reproduce the experiments?
   - A: [Yes/No/Not Applicable]
   - Justification: [Your justification here]

7. **Experiment Statistical Significance**
   - Q: Does the paper report error bars suitably and correctly defined or other appropriate measures of uncertainty in the experimental results?
   - A: [Yes/No/Not Applicable]
   - Justification: [Your justification here]

8. **Experiments Compute Resources**
   - Q: For each experiment, does the paper provide sufficient information on the computing resources used to complete the experiment (e.g., CPU and GPU types, memory, and runtime)?
