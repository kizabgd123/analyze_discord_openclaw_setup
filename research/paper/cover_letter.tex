\noindent\textbf{To the Program Committee:}

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent Please find enclosed our submission titled ``Discord OpenClaw: A Framework for Safe, Proactive AI Agents in Communication Platforms'' for your consideration.

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent This paper presents a novel framework for deploying safe, proactive AI agents in real-time communication platforms. Our key contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item A comprehensive framework that uniquely combines proactive behavior, structured human-in-the-loop approval workflows, and external reasoning integration to address the fundamental tension between helpfulness and safety in AI agents.
    
    \item Empirical evidence from controlled experiments with 110 Discord users demonstrating that our framework significantly improves user engagement (77.1\% increase), safety and trust (53.6\% and 41.9\% increases), and factual accuracy (30.9\% improvement) while reducing hallucinations by 68.2\%.
    
    \item Ablation studies showing that each component contributes uniquely to overall performance, with proactive behavior and approval workflows having the largest individual impacts.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5cm}

\noindent We believe this work is particularly timely given the rapid deployment of AI agents in collaborative environments and the growing need for safety mechanisms that do not compromise utility. Our framework provides both practical guidance for developers and empirical evidence for researchers studying human-AI interaction.
