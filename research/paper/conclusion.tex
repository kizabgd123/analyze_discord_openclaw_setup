We have presented Discord OpenClaw, a framework for safe, proactive AI agents in communication platforms that integrates proactive behavior, structured approval workflows, and external reasoning integration. Our controlled experiments with 110 Discord users demonstrate that this framework significantly improves user engagement (77.1% increase), safety and trust (53.6% and 41.9% increases), and factual accuracy (30.9% improvement) while reducing hallucinations by 68.2%.

The ablation study confirms that each component contributes uniquely to overall performance, with proactive behavior and approval workflows having the largest individual impacts. This suggests that the combination of initiative and oversight creates a synergistic effect that is greater than the sum of its parts.

Our work provides both a practical framework for building safe AI agents in communication platforms and empirical evidence supporting the importance of balancing initiative with oversight. As AI agents become more prevalent in interactive environments, frameworks like Discord OpenClaw will be essential for ensuring that these agents are both helpful and trustworthy.

Future work could explore extending this framework to other communication platforms, investigating the long-term effects of proactive behavior, studying different approval workflow designs, and examining how the framework performs with various external reasoning engines. Additionally, investigating how user characteristics and community norms affect the optimal configuration of such systems could provide valuable insights for personalized deployment.
