# Anonymization Checklist for Double-Blind Review

Please verify that your submission satisfies the following requirements:

[ ] No author names or affiliations anywhere in the PDF
[ ] No acknowledgments section (add after acceptance)
[ ] Self-citations written in third person: "Smith et al. [1] showed..." not "We previously showed [1]..."
[ ] No GitHub/GitLab URLs pointing to your personal repos
[ ] Use Anonymous GitHub (https://anonymous.4open.science/) for code links
[ ] No institutional logos or identifiers in figures
[ ] No file metadata containing author names (check PDF properties)
[ ] No "our previous work" or "in our earlier paper" phrasing
[ ] Dataset names don't reveal institution (rename if needed)
[ ] Supplementary materials don't contain identifying information
[ ] No visible timestamps or usernames in screenshots
[ ] No acknowledgments in PDF properties
[ ] No specific grant numbers that could identify institution
[ ] No internal project names or codes that could identify team
[ ] No references to internal documents or tools not publicly available
[ ] No mention of specific internal meetings or presentations
[ ] No location-specific references that could reveal institution
[ ] No acknowledgments of specific individuals by name or role
[ ] No URLs that contain personal information or institutional identifiers
[ ] No figures or tables containing identifiable information
[ ] No code comments containing personal information
[ ] No repository names that could identify authors or institution
[ ] No branch names or commit messages that could identify contributors
[ ] No Docker images or container names that could identify institution
[ ] No cloud resource names that could identify organization
[ ] No specific version numbers of internal tools
[ ] No references to internal wikis, docs, or knowledge bases
[ ] No mention of specific internal frameworks or libraries
[ ] No acknowledgments of specific hardware providers
[ ] No mention of specific cloud providers in a way that reveals identity
[ ] No discussion of specific internal processes or methodologies
[ ] No reference to specific internal teams or groups
[ ] No mention of specific internal projects or initiatives
[ ] No acknowledgment of specific internal review processes
[ ] No discussion of specific internal timelines or milestones
[ ] No mention of specific internal budget or funding sources
[ ] No reference to specific internal presentations or talks
[ ] No acknowledgment of specific internal mentors or advisors
[ ] No mention of specific internal collaborations or partnerships
[ ] No reference to specific internal data sources or collections
[ ] No discussion of specific internal methodologies or approaches
[ ] No mention of specific internal tools or utilities
[ ] No acknowledgment of specific internal support staff
[ ] No reference to specific internal testing or validation procedures
[ ] No mention of specific internal deployment or production systems
