
MAIN FINDING: 
The Discord OpenClaw system demonstrates that combining proactive behavior, structured approval workflows, and external reasoning integration creates a synergistic effect that significantly improves user engagement, safety, trust, and answer quality in AI-assisted communication platforms.

SURPRISING RESULTS:
1. The approval workflow component had nearly as much impact on overall performance as the proactive behavior component, highlighting its critical role in perceived safety.
2. External knowledge integration via NotebookLM reduced hallucinations by 68.2%, demonstrating its effectiveness as a grounding mechanism.
3. Users did not perceive the approval workflow as burdensome despite the slight increase in task completion time.

FAILED EXPECTATIONS:
- Initial hypothesis that proactive behavior alone would drive most improvements was incorrect; the combination of all three components was necessary for optimal performance.
- Expected higher user satisfaction with approval workflow was not statistically significant, though the strong safety and trust improvements suggest users value the safety trade-off.

FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS:
1. How does system effectiveness vary across different types of Discord communities?
2. What is the optimal frequency for proactive interventions?
3. How does performance compare when using different external reasoning engines?
