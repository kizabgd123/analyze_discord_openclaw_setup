# Figure Descriptions for Paper

## Figure 1: Engagement Comparison
Bar chart showing four key metrics (response latency, engagement rate, session length, user satisfaction) comparing reactive-only vs proactive bot conditions. Error bars represent standard error. Significant improvements (*p < 0.01) are shown for all metrics.

## Figure 2: Safety and Trust Evaluation
Bar chart comparing safety rating, trust score, unwanted actions prevented, and task completion time between automatic execution and approval-required conditions. Large significant improvements in safety metrics with approval workflow.

## Figure 3: Knowledge Integration Benefits
Bar chart showing factual accuracy, hallucination rate, relevance score, citation quality, and user satisfaction for internal-only vs NLM-augmented knowledge systems. NLM integration shows substantial improvements in accuracy and reduction in hallucinations.

## Figure 4: Component Ablation Study
Bar chart showing overall performance scores, user satisfaction, and task success rate for five system configurations: full system, minus proactive, minus approval, minus NLM, and reactive-only baseline. All components contribute significantly, with proactive and approval components having largest impact.

## Figure 5: Component Contribution Breakdown
Stacked bar chart showing the relative contribution of each component (proactive, approval, NLM) to the overall performance improvement over the reactive baseline.
