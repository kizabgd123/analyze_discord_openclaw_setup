# Discoveries Directory

Stores structured discovery artifacts across the research lifecycle:

## Stage 1: Independent Discovery
- `kiza_discoveries.md`: Independent findings, citations, and initial hypotheses by Researcher A.
- `kreten_discoveries.md`: Independent findings, citations, and initial hypotheses by Researcher B.
*Note: Generated in complete isolation with zero peer cross-contamination.*

## Stage 2: Reciprocal Research Relay
- `relay_kiza.md`: Researcher A reads Researcher B's findings and extends them with new literature, mathematical insights, and experiment proposals.
- `relay_kreten.md`: Researcher B reads Researcher A's findings and extends them with new literature, regularization methods, and experiment proposals.
*Note: Strictly non-adversarial knowledge extension.*
